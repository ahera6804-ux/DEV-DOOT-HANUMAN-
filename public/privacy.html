Privacy and safety notice

DEV DOOT HANUMAN is currently a pilot prototype for coordinating community assistance. It is not an official emergency response service.

Information submitted

When you submit a request, the app may store your description, contact details, area, optional location coordinates, optional trusted contact, optional photos, request status and timestamps. Provider registrations and safety reports are also stored.

Who can see it?

In this prototype, information is stored on the computer running the server and is visible to anyone who can access that computer's project data, and to coordinators who can log into the dashboard. Do not use real sensitive personal data until secure hosting, database access controls and retention/deletion processes are configured.

Photos and location

Photos and precise location are optional. Only upload a person's photo with their permission. Location is requested only when you press the location button. You can type an approximate area instead.

Do not submit

Never submit Aadhaar numbers, OTPs, passwords, bank details, or unnecessary medical information.

Emergency help

For an immediate emergency in India, call 112. Do not wait for this app to respond.

Before public launch

The operator must publish real contact details, configure secure authentication and persistent storage, define data retention and deletion, arrange backups and monitoring, and review applicable privacy and consumer requirements. No data deletion request workflow is automated in this prototype yet.
